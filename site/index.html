      How it works
      
    
      
      
      How do the puzzles work?
      The grid has ten zones of ten rows each. Each zone drops a puzzle on-chain and on X: ciphers, codes and crypto history, easy at first and harder later. Type your answer in the Vault. If it is right, plots in that zone unlock for your wallet straight away. Twenty-four hours later the zone opens to everyone.
      Answers ignore capitals, spaces and punctuation. Only a hash of each answer is on-chain, stretched so it cannot be cracked by guessing fast.
      
      
      What is the Golden Punk?
      A one-of-one punk, token #10000, outside the grid. Its puzzle drops after the last zone and builds on all ten answers. The first wallet to solve it claims it for free.
      
      
      What do I actually choose?
      The plot. Each of the 10,000 plots is one NFT, and its token number is its position (plot x, y has id y × 100 + x). Pick a corner, your birthday, a spot next to a friend, or the dead centre.
      
      
      Who decides the punk?
      The contract. When your mint lands it draws one punk at random from all the punks nobody has found yet, so no punk can appear twice and no plot is rarer than another before it is claimed. Minting from another contract is blocked, so nobody can retry until they get a rare one.
      
      
      What does it cost?
      0.0003 ETH per plot, plus network gas, which on Robinhood Chain is a tiny fraction of that. You can claim up to 20 plots in one transaction.
      
      
      Which wallet do I need?
      Any EVM wallet in your browser (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, and similar) with a little ETH on Robinhood Chain. The site asks your wallet to add the network if it is missing.
      
      
      Can I check the set was fixed in advance?
      Yes. All 10,000 punks are in traits.json, and its sha256 hash is stored in the contract's provenance field before the mint opens.
