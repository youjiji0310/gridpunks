faq

what if…
What if the curve never sells out?

Then there is no PancakeSwap pool. Your punks stay yours and you can trade them as NFTs on any BNB Chain marketplace. The pool half stays locked in the contract until the last curve punk sells.

Can I get my BNB back?

No. There is no sell-back to the curve. You can sell your punks to other people as NFTs, or on PancakeSwap after graduation.

Can the team take the pool BNB?

No. Half of each payment is booked for the team and half for the pool. The contract only lets the team withdraw its own half. The pool half can only go into the PancakeSwap pool.

Why does the team get half?

That is how the project is funded, and it is written in the contract and on this page before you mint. The other half makes sure the token has real liquidity from the first second on PancakeSwap.

Can I transfer or list my punks before graduation?

Yes. Punks are normal NFTs (ERC-721 mirror) and the units are a normal token (ERC-20). The only blocked move is sending units into the pool pair before graduation.

Why agents only?

Bots and sniping scripts normally take everything. Here only wallets with a Binance Agent ID can buy on the curve, and each wallet is capped at 100 punks.

for agents

setup · once
  1. Binance App → Agentic Wallet: turn on Developer Mode (needed for any contract call).
  2. Claim a .ai Agent ID in Binance Wallet (Agent ID banner). It's bound to your Agentic Wallet.
  3. Install the Binance Agentic Wallet CLI:
    npm i -g @binance/agentic-wallet
  4. Get the AgentPunks CLI (one file, no install):
    curl -O https://SITE/punks.mjs
  5. Give your agent the skill:
    https://SITE/skill.md
  6. Sign in:
    baw auth signin
  7. Check everything:
    node punks.mjs preflight
